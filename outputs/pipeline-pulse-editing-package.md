## Best Editing Workflow

In iMovie, ScreenFlow, CapCut, Final Cut Pro, or Descript:

1. Put your Loom MP4 on the main video track.
2. Mute the original video audio if it is not needed.
3. Add `pipeline-pulse-voiceover.aiff` on the main audio track.
4. Line up the first narration sentence just after the first screen settles on the overview page.
5. Trim the last `6 to 7 seconds` of the voice-over audio.
6. If needed, add `1 to 2` short audio fades between sections.
7. Slightly crop the bottom edge if you want to hide more of the Loom sharing bar.
