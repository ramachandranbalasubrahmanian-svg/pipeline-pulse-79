# Pipeline Pulse 100-Second Demo Script

## Goal

This is a 100-second portfolio demo for **Pipeline Pulse**, positioned as a **DAMA DM-BOK aligned enterprise data platform simulation**. The story should sound confident, executive-friendly, and practical: this is not just a dashboard, it is a control plane for trust, governance, quality, access, AI risk, and business value.

## Recording Note

Use your local browser and record at `http://127.0.0.1:8080/`.

Recommended pages for this demo:

1. `/`
2. `/dama`
3. `/dq`
4. `/access`
5. `/governance`
6. `/ai-governance`
7. `/value`

## 100-Second Narration

### 0-10 seconds: Overview

Open `/`

Say:

“Pipeline Pulse is my portfolio simulation of an enterprise data platform built around the DAMA philosophy. The core idea is simple: data management should not live in disconnected tools. Governance, quality, security, lineage, AI controls, and business value need to be visible in one operating model.”

### 10-28 seconds: DAMA Control Tower

Open `/dama`

Say:

“This control tower is the strategic view. I designed the platform around all 14 DAMA pillars, not as theory, but as demonstrable capability areas. You can see that every module is scored above 95 in this simulation, with visible evidence, persistence, and a clear demo path behind each pillar.”

### 28-43 seconds: Data Quality

Open `/dq`

Say:

“One key design decision was to make the platform executable, not just descriptive. In Data Quality, I can run validation across a synthetic 1,000-record batch, generate evidence IDs, and keep run history. That makes the platform feel audit-ready instead of just presentational.”

### 43-58 seconds: Access Control

Open `/access`

Click `Policy Simulator`

Say:

“Another important philosophy here is policy-driven trust. Access is handled through both RBAC and ABAC, so permissions are not just role-based, they also consider tenant, region, sensitivity, and MFA posture. That gives the demo a stronger enterprise security story.”

### 58-72 seconds: Governance

Open `/governance`

Say:

“Governance is treated as a lifecycle, not a document repository. Policies have owners, stewards, approval stages, review cycles, and evidence. That is important because DAMA is really about operationalizing accountability, not only cataloging assets.”

### 72-84 seconds: AI Governance

Open `/ai-governance`

Say:

“I also extended the platform into AI governance, because modern data platforms are incomplete without model controls. This section shows approval gates, drift and fairness thresholds, and model review evidence, so the platform covers both classic DAMA controls and newer AI risk expectations.”

### 84-100 seconds: Value Realization

Open `/value`

Optionally click `Save Value Snapshot`

Say:

“And finally, the platform ends with value realization. That was another intentional decision: data programs should connect governance and quality back to measurable outcomes. Here I show persisted value snapshots, trust scores, compliance readiness, and executive-facing impact. So the platform is not only detailed across DAMA’s 14 pillars, it also explains why those pillars matter to business decisions.”

## Short Closing Line

Use this if you want a sharper ending:

“Pipeline Pulse is my view of what a modern DAMA-aligned data platform should look like: governed, measurable, policy-driven, and executive-readable.”

## What To Click During The Demo

- On `/`: pause briefly on the landing overview
- On `/dama`: keep the scorecard visible
- On `/dq`: show the executable validation area and run history
- On `/access`: open `Policy Simulator`
- On `/governance`: show lifecycle evidence
- On `/ai-governance`: show approval gates
- On `/value`: show persisted value evidence and optionally save a new snapshot

## Main Talking Points

- This is a **portfolio-range simulation**, not a claim of production certification
- The platform is organized around **DAMA’s 14 pillars**
- The philosophy is **one control plane instead of fragmented tooling**
- The design decision is **executable evidence over static slides**
- The security model is **RBAC + ABAC + tenant and sensitivity awareness**
- The business framing is **trust, auditability, and value realization**
- Local persistence exists now, with optional **Supabase free-tier mirroring**

## Delivery Style

- Speak steadily, not fast
- Do not explain every UI card
- Keep the narrative at platform level
- Use “designed,” “operationalized,” “evidence,” and “decision-making” language
- Treat DAMA as the structure and value realization as the payoff
